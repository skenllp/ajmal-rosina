Our Story

Two Families, One Journey

Every beautiful beginning starts with a moment of faith, family blessings, and the grace of Almighty Allah. As two families come together in love and tradition, Muhammed Ajmal and Rosina Thesni begin a new chapter filled with hope, happiness, and lifelong companionship. Surrounded by the warmth of their loved ones, they look forward to a journey built on mutual respect, cherished memories, and heartfelt prayers.

How It Began

Two families met, two hearts aligned, and a lifelong journey began. What started as a meeting between two families soon became a bond strengthened by shared values, warm conversations, and the blessings of elders. With each passing day, the foundation for a beautiful future grew stronger.

The Blessing

Held together by faith, bound by love, and forever sealed in a sacred vow — the Nikah of Ajmal and Rosina was solemnised with the love and guidance of their families, embraced with joy and gratitude by the grace of Almighty Allah.

On Saturday, 14 November 2026, from 3:00 PM to 7:00 PM at AJWA Auditorium, Muhammed Ajmal and Rosina Thesni celebrate their wedding and reception. As they step into this new chapter (Insha Allah), they seek the blessings, love, and prayers of everyone who joins them in celebrating this special occasion.
